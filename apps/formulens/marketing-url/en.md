# Know exactly what's in your skincare.

**Snap a photo of any ingredient list and get a clear, color-coded breakdown of what everything does — then compare products or check whether your whole routine actually works together. All in seconds, all on your device.**

Ingredient labels are written for chemists, not for you. FormuLens translates them. Point your camera at any product, and every ingredient is decoded and sorted by what it actually does — no typing, no searching, no chemistry degree.

[![Download on the App Store](/assets/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/formulalens/id6756229042)

## Why you'll love it

- **Understand any label in seconds** — see what each ingredient does, from humectants and actives to preservatives and fragrance, without looking anything up.
- **Shop smarter** — FormuLens highlights the ingredients that matter for each product type and flags anything unusual, so you can spot a thoughtful formula before you buy.
- **Compare products head-to-head** — put two products side by side to see shared and unique ingredients, how the formulas differ, and a plain-language verdict on which fits what.
- **Make your routine work together** — get the right layering order, catch conflicts between steps, spot redundant overlap, and find the gaps you're missing.

## Three ways to analyze

- **Compare** — scan several products of the same type and see how they stack up at a glance.
- **Check My Routine** — scan your routine in order for layering guidance, conflict alerts, overlap notes, and gap analysis.
- **Scan Collection** — scan any mix of products for a clean, per-product breakdown, no comparison or ordering.

## How it works

1. **Pick your goal** — Compare, Check My Routine, or Scan Collection.
2. **Choose your categories** — 23 product types across Skincare, Makeup, Hair, Bath & Body, and Fragrance.
3. **Snap or import** — photograph the label or pick from your library. Long list? Capture it across a few photos and FormuLens merges them into one.
4. **Read your results** — every ingredient is color-coded by function, key ingredients starred, unusual ones flagged. Sunscreens show their Active and Inactive sections just like the Drug Facts label.
5. **Go deeper** — tap any ingredient for a plain-language explanation and links to trusted databases (INCIDecoder, COSMILE Europe, EWG Skin Deep).

Save any analysis with a custom name, tags, and notes, then search and filter anytime. Perfect for tracking what worked, comparing across shopping trips, or remembering that one serum you loved.
