# How can we help?

We want you to get the most out of LinkHQ. If something isn't working as expected or you have a question, you'll likely find the answer below. If not, reach out and we'll help.

## Getting Started

### How do I set up the app?

Just open LinkHQ. There's no account and no sign-up. A welcome screen explains the idea with a short three-step guide, and once you tap to enter your library you're ready to start saving links.

### How do I save a link?

Find something you want to keep in another app, tap Share, and choose LinkHQ in the share sheet, the same way you'd send it to a friend. Adjust the title, pick a source, add a note and tags if you like, then tap Save. You can also paste a link with Add Link, from the + button in the app.

### What happens after I save a link?

LinkHQ fills in the real title and preview image of the video, post, or page, picks out a few keywords, and sorts the link into a topic. If you've already edited the title, LinkHQ keeps yours. A link saved while you're offline gets its details the next time you open the app with a connection.

### How do I find a link again?

Search from the top of the Library or Favorites list. It looks through titles, URLs, notes, tags, and keywords, suggests matching keywords as you type, and doesn't mind capitals or accents. You can also open the Categories tab and browse by topic, source, tag, or keyword, then narrow the list down by favorites, unopened, category, source, or keyword.

### How do I reopen a link at its source?

Open a link and tap Open Original, or swipe the row to Open. The video or post opens in its own app when it's installed, so a YouTube link opens YouTube and a RedNote link opens the note in RedNote. Links without an app open in your browser. Opening a link also marks it as opened.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is LinkHQ free?

Yes. LinkHQ is free to download and use, with no ads and no account. We may introduce optional premium features in the future.

### Does LinkHQ collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Your library is stored only on your device and is never uploaded. See our full [Privacy Policy](https://bartlettbutter.com/linkhq/privacy/) for details.

### How does LinkHQ sort my links into categories?

Every link is sorted into one of 16 topics, such as Food & Drink, Travel & Places, Technology, and Music. With Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple's on-device model does the sorting. Otherwise, LinkHQ sorts by the link's title, tags, and keywords. Both happen right on your device. The footer under the categories tells you which one sorted your links.

### Can I change a link's category?

Yes. Open the link and pick a different category. LinkHQ keeps your choice and won't sort that link again.

### Which platforms does LinkHQ recognize?

LinkHQ recognizes 32 of the biggest platforms and labels each link with its own icon and color, including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp, WeChat, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, Spotify, Bilibili, RedNote, Weibo, GitHub, Medium, Apple Music, and Apple Podcasts. Links from any other site are saved too, grouped under Web. You can change a link's source yourself at any time.

### What are unopened links?

A link you haven't reopened through LinkHQ yet carries a small dot, and the Unopened filter gathers them in one place. Opening a link marks it opened, and you can also mark a link opened or unopened by swiping its row.

### What does "Unavailable" mean?

When you open your library, LinkHQ checks whether your saved links are still there. Each link is checked at most once every 12 hours. A link whose content has been removed is labeled Unavailable, and you can find all of them in Categories. Tap Check Again on a link's details to check it right away. If the content comes back, the label goes away. A link that needs sign-in or that couldn't be reached is never marked Unavailable on that basis alone.

### What does a duplicate warning mean?

If you share or add a link that's already saved, LinkHQ shows when and under what title you saved it before. Saving it again is still allowed. The warning is just there so you can decide.

### How do Favorites work?

Turn on Favorite in a link's details, or swipe its row, to add it to Favorites. Your starred links have their own Favorites tab.

### Can I use LinkHQ in Dark Mode?

Yes. In Settings, under Appearance, choose System, Light, or Dark. Your choice also applies when you save from the share sheet.

### Does LinkHQ work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad, a sidebar organizes your library by status, topic, source, and tag, each with its count, and a link's details open beside the list when there's room. With a keyboard, ⌘N adds a link and ⌘, opens Settings.

### Does LinkHQ support larger text sizes?

Yes. All text follows your device's text-size setting, up to the largest accessibility sizes.

### How do I rate LinkHQ?

Go to Settings and tap Rate LinkHQ. It opens LinkHQ's App Store page, where you can leave a rating and, if you like, a review. Thank you, it really helps.

### How do I clear my whole library?

Go to Settings and tap Delete All Links. After you confirm, every saved link is removed from your device. This can't be undone.

## Permissions

### Does LinkHQ need any special permissions?

No. LinkHQ does not request the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, health data, or location. Saving links from the share sheet and reopening them at their source don't need those permissions.

### Why does LinkHQ use the network?

Only to look up the links you save. To show a link's title and preview, and to check that it's still available, LinkHQ asks the link's own site, the same request your browser would make, and loads the preview image from wherever that site points. Keywords and topic sorting happen on your device. Your library itself is never uploaded.

## Troubleshooting

### A link didn't get a real title

Some sites require sign-in to view their content, so LinkHQ can only use the text that was shared with the link, and a link pasted in by hand keeps its web address as the title until you edit it. Some sites also limit how often they can be looked up. LinkHQ tries again the next time you open the app, so a missing title often fills in on its own. You can always edit the title yourself.

### A saved link doesn't appear right away

A link saved from the share sheet while LinkHQ is open in the background may not appear until you close and reopen the app.

### Open Original opened the browser instead of the app

A link opens in its own app only when that app is installed and supports opening that kind of link. Some links, such as WeChat articles, always open in the browser. Otherwise, check that the app is installed and up to date.

### A thumbnail isn't showing

Some platforms, such as Instagram and TikTok, use preview images that expire, and some don't share previews without sign-in, so those links show the platform's icon instead. This doesn't affect saving or reopening the link.

### A duplicate wasn't caught for a short link

A short link (such as one from bit.ly or a share button) is matched with the link it points to once LinkHQ has looked it up. Opening the app with a connection gives it a chance to do that.

### The app is crashing

Make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store and restart your device. If crashes persist, please email us with your device model and iOS version so we can investigate.
