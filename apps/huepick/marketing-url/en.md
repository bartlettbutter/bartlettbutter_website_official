# See colors differently.

Snap a photo of anything that inspires you. Huepick instantly reveals the hidden palette inside and gives you creative tools to do something beautiful with it. Completely free, completely private.

[![Download on the App Store](/assets/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg)](https://apps.apple.com/app/huepick)

## What you'll actually do with Huepick

- **Find the perfect palette in seconds.** Point your camera at a sunset, a mural, or a fabric swatch, and Huepick pulls out a 12-color palette you can use immediately. Tap any swatch to copy its hex code. No more eyeballing colors or guessing what's in the frame.
- **Understand the colors you captured.** Huepick breaks down your palette with clear visual analysis: hue distribution, warm-vs-cool temperature, lightness and contrast, saturation, and a full color breakdown. See what makes an image work.
- **Transform your photos into art.** Six creative tools (Isolate, Duotone, Tritone, Pixelize, Blur, and Glitch) let you turn any photo into something shareable, using colors from your own palette.
- **Keep everything private and offline.** Your photos never leave your device. No sign-ups, no cloud uploads, no tracking. Works anywhere, even without Wi-Fi.

## How it works

1. **Snap or choose a photo** — no account, no setup
2. **See your palette** — up to 12 dominant colors extracted instantly, tap to copy any hex code
3. **Explore the analysis** — hue, temperature, lightness, contrast, and saturation at a glance
4. **Play** — transform your photo with six creative tools, guided by smart suggestions for your image
5. **Save and share** — download creations to your Photos, or share them directly

## What's inside

**Analyze** — Extract a 12-color palette and explore it with detailed visual breakdowns: an overview with diversity score and hue spread, hue distribution, color temperature, lightness and contrast, saturation, and a per-color breakdown. Huepick even recommends which creative tools will work best with your specific photo.

**Playground** — Six tools to transform your image:
- *Isolate* — spotlight specific colors while the rest goes monochrome
- *Duotone* — remap your image to two bold tones
- *Tritone* — remap your image across three tones for richer gradients
- *Pixelize* — convert to a mosaic of geometric tiles
- *Blur* — add soft, dreamy focus with a range of blur styles
- *Glitch* — apply digital distortion for an edgy look

**Gallery** — Every creation you save lives here. Re-edit, download, or share anytime.
