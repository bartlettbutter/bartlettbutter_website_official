About Us

We make apps that respect you

Bartlett Butter is a small, independent studio focused on crafting apps that solve real problems without compromise. Every app works offline and respects your data.

Philosophy

We believe the best apps are the ones that get out of your way. Privacy isn't a feature — it's the foundation. Every design decision starts with the question: does this serve the person using it?

Focus

  • On-Device AI
  • Privacy-First
  • Offline-Capable
  • Beautiful UI
  • Real-Time Data
  • Native Performance

Technology

How We Build

Quality over quantity, always

01

Designed for real life

Every app starts with a genuine problem. We don't build for novelty — we build tools people actually reach for every day.

02

Respects your attention

No dark patterns, no engagement tricks. Our apps do their job and get out of your way, because your time matters more than our metrics.

03

Crafted, not shipped

We sweat the details — from micro-interactions to accessibility. Every screen is considered, refined, and tested until it feels right.

04

Privacy by design

We collect as little data as possible and process on-device whenever we can. Your data is yours — that's not a feature, it's our starting point.
