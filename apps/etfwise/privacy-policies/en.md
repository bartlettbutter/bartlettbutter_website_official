**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

ETFWise ("the App") is an iOS application, free to download with an optional Premium subscription, that provides daily ETF picks with market data and instant on-device analysis. Your privacy is fundamental to how we built this app. Here's exactly how your information is handled.

## The Short Version

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do you collect personal data? | No |
| Are there accounts or logins? | No |
| Is there tracking or analytics? | No |
| Are there ads? | No |
| Is my data sold or shared? | No |
| Does the analysis leave my device? | No |
| Do you receive my payment details? | No. Premium purchases are handled by Apple |
| Can children use the app safely? | Yes |

The only data the App sends to financial data providers is ETF ticker symbols and date ranges to fetch market data. Premium purchases are processed by Apple.

## What Data We Collect

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. ETFWise has no user accounts, no usage analytics, and no advertising or tracking frameworks.

## What Stays on Your Device

All app data is stored locally on your device:

- Daily ETF recommendations and cached market prices
- News sentiment scores and generated analysis
- Your settings, such as appearance and performance window
- Your Screener rules and match mode

Market data is refreshed regularly and none of this data is uploaded to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

ETFWise connects to third-party financial data providers solely to fetch market data for display.

| Data Sent | Personal Data Included |
|---|---|
| ETF ticker symbols and date ranges | None |

No device identifiers, location, or tracking information is included in any request.

## Premium Subscription

ETFWise offers an optional auto-renewing Premium subscription that unlocks the Screener. Purchases, billing, renewals, restores, and cancellations are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information, and the App has no accounts of its own. The App checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's purchase records. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Apple's handling of your data is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## On-Device Analysis

The bull cases, risk cases, and fund descriptions are generated entirely on your device from the market data already fetched for each ETF. This analysis involves no separate download and no external server. Nothing about it is sent off your device.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. We have no data-sharing agreements, no advertising partners, and no analytics providers.

## Permissions

ETFWise does not request access to your camera, location, contacts, microphone, health data, photos, or App Tracking Transparency. No special permissions are required to use the app.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Since we collect no personal data on our servers, there is nothing to retain or delete on our end. All locally stored data, including recommendations, cached prices, settings, Screener rules, and generated analysis, is permanently removed when you delete the app from your device. Deleting the app does not cancel a Premium subscription; manage that in your Apple Account settings.

## Your Rights

Because ETFWise does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete from our systems. You maintain full control over all locally stored data by managing or removing the app on your device.

## Children's Privacy

ETFWise does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Because the app collects no personal data whatsoever, it complies with COPPA requirements.

## International Users

Because ETFWise collects no personal data, no cross-border transfer of personal information occurs. Ticker symbol requests to financial data providers contain no personal data or device identifiers regardless of your location.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
