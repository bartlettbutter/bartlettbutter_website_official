# How can we help?

We build TrafficVibe to make your mornings calmer, and we want using it to feel the same way. Most questions are answered below — and if yours isn't, a real person is one email away and always happy to help.

## Getting Started

### What is the morning briefing?

When you open TrafficVibe, the first thing you see is a plain-language read of your commute with the most important issue called out first — how the roads are running, what's slowing you down, and whether now is a good time to leave. There are no dials to decode; it just tells you what your drive looks like and what to do about it.

### How does TrafficVibe know the best time to leave?

The app builds an hourly outlook of how your drive changes through the day and compares it against a typical baseline, then highlights the usual rush-hour windows. When waiting a few minutes means a meaningfully faster trip, it says so, so you can pick your smoothest departure.

### Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, for live traffic, weather, and map data. If you're offline, TrafficVibe shows the last cached reading with a "last updated" badge rather than a blank screen.

### How much does TrafficVibe cost?

TrafficVibe is free from the App Store. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

## Commute Life Indices

### What do the colors mean?

Every index reads at a glance: green for good or clear, yellow for caution or moderate, red for bad or act now. A given color means the same thing in every city — only the wording of the advice adapts to your situation.

### Why does an index say "no data"?

TrafficVibe never invents a number. When something genuinely can't be measured for the city you're viewing, that badge reads "no data" instead of showing a made-up rating. Availability varies by place — for example, fuel prices depend on a published price for your country, and some road-feature badges depend on how thoroughly your area is mapped. It's not an error; it's the app being honest.

### What are the nineteen indices?

They're grouped by what a commuter actually asks:

| Group | Indices |
|---|---|
| Should I leave now? | Highway Speed, Rush Hour |
| What's slowing me down? | Incidents, Congestion, Detour, Delay |
| Weather on the road | Weather, Ice, Visibility, Sun Glare |
| Planning & context | Fuel Price, Road Load, Fuel Timing |
| Along the way | Tolls, Rail Crossing, Lighting, Speed Cameras, EV Charging, Bike Share |

### Where does the data come from?

TrafficVibe draws on trusted, established mapping, weather, and public-data sources for routing and ETAs, conditions, nearby road features, holidays, and fuel prices (shown in your local currency). Your briefing and its ratings are put together on your device — nothing about your commute is uploaded. For exactly what leaves your device and why, see our [Privacy Policy](https://www.bartlettbutter.com/trafficvibe/privacy/).

## Notifications

### What alerts can I get?

Departure reminders, delay-spike warnings, and incident alerts. You control which ones are on from Settings.

### How do I stop being nagged?

Set your own quiet hours and turn on the weekdays-only preference. TrafficVibe also remembers what it has already told you, so it won't repeat the same warning twice.

### Why aren't my alerts firing?

TrafficVibe doesn't refresh in the background — it evaluates alerts when you open the app or pull to refresh. Make sure notifications are enabled in Settings, and that you're not inside your quiet hours or a weekday-only exclusion.

## Commute Cities

### How do commute cities work?

Add the places you travel to from the cities sheet on the dashboard. Each one becomes a route from your current city, unlocking door-to-door ETAs, delay alerts, and departure reminders. Switching between saved cities is instant.

### Why can't I add my current city as a destination?

A city that matches your current location is ignored, because a route from a place to itself has nothing to show.

## Preferences

### Can I change units and time format?

Yes. Settings holds one measurement-system choice (US Customary or metric) that governs distance, speed, and temperature together, plus a 12- or 24-hour time toggle. Appearance follows your system Light or Dark setting.

## Troubleshooting

### Conditions aren't loading

Check that your device is connected to the internet, then pull down to refresh. If a source is temporarily unavailable, TrafficVibe serves the last cached reading with a "last updated" badge and retries on the next refresh.

### A metric shows "no data"

This is by design when a source isn't available for the selected city (see "Why does an index say 'no data'?" above). It is not an error — the app is simply refusing to guess.

### The app is crashing or freezing

Make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store, then force-close the app and reopen it. If the problem continues, restart your device. If it still persists, please contact us below.
