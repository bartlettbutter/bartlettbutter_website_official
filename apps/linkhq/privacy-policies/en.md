**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

LinkHQ ("the App") is a free link library for iPhone and iPad that keeps everything on your device. Privacy is central to how we built it. This policy explains exactly what data is involved when you use LinkHQ and, more importantly, what isn't.

## The Short Version

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do you collect personal data? | No |
| Is my library sent to your servers? | No |
| Are there accounts or logins? | No |
| Is there tracking or analytics? | No |
| Are there ads? | No |
| Is my data sold or shared? | No |
| Do you collect data from children? | No |

The links you save, their titles, notes, tags, keywords, and categories all stay on your device. We operate no servers that receive your data. LinkHQ uses the network only to look up the links you save, as described below.

## What Data Is Collected

**None.** LinkHQ has no user accounts, no analytics platform, no advertising SDK, and no tracking mechanism of any kind. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

## How the App Uses Data Locally

### Your Saved Links

The links you save, with their titles, notes, tags, keywords, categories, source, favorites, and opened and availability status, are stored only on your device, in a container shared by the app and its share sheet extension. They are never uploaded.

### Saving from the Share Sheet

When you share something to LinkHQ, it reads the shared link, or a link found inside shared text, so it can be saved. This happens on your device. Nothing about the share is sent to any server we control.

### On-Device Keywords

When a link is saved, a few keywords describing its content are picked out on your device, for search and filtering. This uses Apple's on-device language frameworks. No text is sent anywhere for this.

### On-Device Topic Sorting

LinkHQ sorts each link into a topic, such as Food & Drink or Travel & Places. On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, this uses Apple's on-device foundation model; otherwise LinkHQ sorts by the link's title, tags, keywords, and note. Both run entirely on your device, and nothing about your links is sent anywhere for this.

### Preview Images

Preview images are cached on your device so they don't have to be downloaded again each time they're shown. The cache is managed by iOS and is removed with the app.

### Settings and Rating Prompt

Your appearance choice (System, Light, or Dark) is stored on your device. To avoid asking too often, LinkHQ also keeps a small local record of when it last asked you to rate the app, and whether you opened the App Store review page from Settings. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple's App Store, not by us. This record never leaves your device.

## How LinkHQ Uses the Network

To show a link's real title and preview image instead of a bare URL, and to tell you when a link's content has been removed, LinkHQ looks up the links you save. Each lookup concerns a single link, never your collection.

- **Titles, previews, and keywords.** When a link is saved, and later in the app if that didn't finish, LinkHQ requests the link from **its own site**. For some platforms it asks that platform's **public embed or data service** instead (for example YouTube, TikTok, X, Bilibili, or Wikipedia), the same sources that power embedded posts on other websites. A short link (such as bit.ly) is followed through the service that issued it to find where it points.
- **Preview images** are loaded from wherever the link's site points, which may be an image server run by that site.
- **Availability checks.** Each time you open your library, LinkHQ checks whether your saved links are still available by asking each link's own site, or that platform's public service, in the same way. You can also check a single link with Check Again.
- These requests are made directly from your device, the same requests your browser would make for that link. They are not sent to any server operated by us, and they carry no account or personal identifier, because there is none.
- **Your library is never uploaded.** Your notes, tags, keywords, categories, and favorites are never sent anywhere.

A lookup that can't reach a site, such as when you're offline, is simply tried again on a later launch.

## Permissions

LinkHQ does not request any sensitive device permissions.

| Permission | Status |
|------------|--------|
| **Microphone** | Not requested |
| **Camera** | Not requested |
| **Contacts** | Not requested |
| **Photos** | Not requested |
| **Health data** | Not requested |
| **Location** | Not requested |

Saving links from the share sheet and reopening them at their source do not require any of these permissions.

## Third-Party Services

LinkHQ does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Link lookups contact each link's own site, or that platform's public service, directly. Keywords and topic sorting use Apple frameworks that run as part of the operating system on your device. Websites you reach through LinkHQ, including when you tap Open Original, have their own privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, license, or share your data with anyone. LinkHQ never transmits your library, notes, tags, keywords, or categories.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We store no data on our servers, so there is nothing to retain or delete on our end. Everything on your device, your saved links and all their details, is removed the moment you delete the app. You can also clear your library in place with Settings › Delete All Links.

## Your Rights

Because we collect nothing, there is no personal data to access, correct, port, or erase on our end. You have complete control over everything stored locally by managing or removing the app.

## Children's Privacy

LinkHQ does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). LinkHQ shows titles and previews from the sites of the links you save, so the content you see depends on the links you choose to keep.

## International Users

Because LinkHQ collects no personal data, no cross-border transfer of personal information occurs. Link lookups reach whichever site hosts the link you saved.
